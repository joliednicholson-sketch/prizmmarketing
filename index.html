About me

Hi, I'm Jolie!

I'm a marketing student at UNC Wilmington with a passion for helping businesses strengthen their brand and connect with their customers in creative and meaningful ways.

Through my experience in sales, marketing, and working with consumer brands, I've developed a strong understanding of how strategy, creativity, and customer experience work together to grow a business. I especially love social media, content creation, branding, and finding new ways for businesses to stand out.

I created my freelance marketing services to help local businesses bring their ideas to life, whether that means building a stronger social media presence, creating engaging content, refreshing a brand, or developing a marketing strategy that actually fits their business.

My approach combines creativity, current trends, and data-driven thinking while keeping the personality behind each business at the center of everything I create.
